# Track 1 data requirements and evidence contract

The current checkpoint requires a short brief, start year, base/option years and selected proposed quantities; detailed operational metadata, experience and qualifications are optional. Current deployment is comparison context, zero excludes a role, and blanks remain unanswered. Earlier table descriptions below do not add a mandatory questionnaire. Versioned cross-agent transfers are documented in [docs/INTERFACES.md](docs/INTERFACES.md); incomplete app drafts and approved budget documents remain distinct.

The revised release uses only labelled synthetic tender fixtures. Do not infer missing real fields or import raw documents automatically. `services/evidence.py:EvidenceRow` is the strict row contract; `services/models.py` defines project, duty, role, coverage, policy and decision contracts.

| Capability | Mandatory inputs | Optional inputs / applicability | Current source and gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| Contract profile | Start date, base years, option years, operational description, locations, hours, critical functions, response needs and asset status | Explicit unknowns retained for operational facts; exclusion list | Officer statements and supporting text, reviewed before confirmation |
| Responsibility coverage | Stable duty ID, description, plan/external/not applicable/unknown, external provider when applicable | Requirement evidence, exclusions | Officer confirmation; no property-type staffing rules |
| Role mapping | Original label, included/excluded decision, duty IDs, mapping rationale for a non-exact title | Exact role/approved alias, experience and qualifications | Eight matched fixture roles; Events Manager and M&E Engineer unmatched |
| Shift quantities | Hours/day, days/week, simultaneous posts, employee capacity, gross/net basis, relief factor, rounding choice, selected quantity and rationale | Year-specific deployment, explicit exceptions | Officer assumptions, deterministic coverage/FTE tool; no verified working-hours policy or feasible roster |
| Non-shift quantities | Selected scenario and operational rationale | Validated workload rules, historical quantities for comparison | No workload rules; historical fixture quantities cannot mandate staffing |
| Rate benchmark | Record/tender/schedule IDs, original/standard role, positive inclusive monthly rate, currency/unit basis, priced year, submitted/awarded status, scope, source, validated status and provenance | Experience, qualifications, inclusions/exclusions, components only if actually sourced | Synthetic inclusive quotes; no real market/qualification premiums or salary breakdown |
| Multiyear escalation | Same-scope schedule ID, role, unit rate and basis, actual contract year/pricing year, contract start/duration, submission year, submitted/awarded status, source and validation | Causes only when established by source; policy inclusion metadata | Ten tenders, seven-year schedules; annual rows are not independent schedule samples |
| Quoted target-year prices | Exactly one comparable rate for every target calendar year, matching selected base quote | Missing years require assumptions instead; calendar quotes require January start in this release | Synthetic complete schedules; no extrapolation or double escalation |
| Component policy change | Policy name, source, effective date, affected component, evidenced absolute monthly increment per head, confirmed conversion and overlap review | Verified policy applicability and source validation | Officer-supplied unverified sources; no automatic policy research or full-inclusive wage increment |
| Budget | Positive quantity/rate, complete role/year escalation/quote schedule, contract periods | Year-specific quantities, included additive component adjustments | Deterministic cent rounding and optional-period separation |
| Findings/decisions | Stable finding ID, role/year, message/impact, blocking flag, status, evidence, inclusion/exclusion, officer justification, timestamp | Further approval required | Current findings plus revisions/previous decisions; invalid inputs cannot be excepted |
| Excel evidence | Confirmed facts, selected inputs, formulas, yearly/contract totals, assumptions, decisions, provenance, status/version/officer | Source URLs/document hashes, charts and sensitivity | Nine exported sections; no invented component build-up |

Rate ranges use linear quantiles and at least three distinct schedules. Average is the arithmetic mean of eligible records deduplicated by schedule/year; mixed priced years do not produce a pooled numeric range. Filtered comparisons retain scope; base-year quotes are not automatically normalised to a target year. Submitted and awarded schedules of one tender remain paired observations, not independent tenders.

Real tender onboarding requires explicit source validation, role mappings, unit/currency normalisation, submitted/awarded distinctions, schedule scope consistency, actual year verification, duplicate review and provenance. Benchmark eligibility must be determined separately from upload extraction. Historical database maintenance is Track 5, outside this release.
