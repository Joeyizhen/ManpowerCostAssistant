## Deterministic PWM method

Official source snapshot `data/reference/pwm-2026-10-19.json` contains 34 explicit monthly full-time categories from MOM cleaning, security, landscape and lift/escalator pages. Each category retains source URL, check date, wage basis, dated points, published-valid-through date and a content digest. Outsourced/in-house and basic/gross wage bases remain distinct. This snapshot is separate from synthetic manpower tender fixtures and is not a market-rate source.

Sources: [cleaning](https://www.mom.gov.sg/employment-practices/progressive-wage-model/cleaning-sector), [security](https://www.mom.gov.sg/employment-practices/progressive-wage-model/security-sector), [landscape](https://www.mom.gov.sg/employment-practices/progressive-wage-model/landscape-sector), [lift/escalator](https://www.mom.gov.sg/employment-practices/progressive-wage-model/lift-and-escalator-sector).

The officer confirms category, applicability, covered headcount, known salary component/source at contract start, flat remaining inclusive charge and any separately evidenced employer on-cost. There is no title-based eligibility inference. For month m: adjusted salary = starting salary × applicable published wage(m) / published wage(contract start), with an explicitly selected unpublished-period factor where relevant. The supplied salary is anchored at contract start: assumptions do not retrospectively uplift it for pre-contract periods. Salary rounds per worker to cents; salary delta × covered workers is summed for 12 calendar months per contract year. Employer on-cost applies only to that delta and is aggregated/rounded separately. These are budgeting conventions, not statutory payroll rounding or a claim that every worker legally requires the schedule's exact percentage rise.

Beyond the published period, hold-last or annual officer assumptions require a meaningful reason. Annual assumptions start in the first unpublished schedule month and repeat annually; when a contract starts later, the factor is normalised to its starting salary. The UI/export labels those months as assumptions. A changed source digest requires reconfirmation. Existing dated absolute component adjustments retain their original daily proration; calendar-month selection applies to PWM.

Independent invented example: three inclusive manpower units at SGD3,000/month, two covered cleaners at supplied salary SGD2,000, Jan2026 start. The July2026 schedule movement 1,910→2,080 yields salary 2,178.01. Six months × two workers ×178.01 = SGD2,136.12; a separately supplied synthetic 10% incremental on-cost adds SGD213.61. Year1 cleaning cost is SGD110,349.73. Year2 salary/on-cost additions are SGD7,049.20. With a separate FM role at SGD8,000/month and 2% Year2 assumption, full/base/option totals are SGD419,318.93 / SGD206,349.73 / SGD212,969.20. No official employer rate is inferred by this example.

## Tender-derived choices and rationale gates

Comparable manpower-only unit-rate schedules provide one within-schedule median YoY observation each. Median, arithmetic Average and Higher reference (P75, linearly interpolated percentile) weight schedules equally. Repeated years do not overweight a tenderer. Actual percentages and resulting base/option/full-role costs are shown. These are quoted price expectations, not observed wage changes; their causes are unknown. No average/median conservatism ordering is asserted. Custom assumptions are explicitly labelled.

Rationale checks are conservative text-completeness heuristics, not semantic verification. They reject plainly generic answers and require a source identifier for “sourced evidence”; sources supplied by an officer are not automatically verified. Old meaningful role explanations remain usable for saved-project compatibility, labelled as officer assumptions. Formal shared bases bind to role-specific value/context signatures. Relevant revisions invalidate confirmations; unrelated price edits preserve a staffing judgement. Invalid arithmetic cannot be bypassed with an exception. Missing rationale blocks that decision's confirmation but does not prevent draft saving/calculation preview.
