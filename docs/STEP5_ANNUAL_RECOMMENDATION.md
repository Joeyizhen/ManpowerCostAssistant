Step 5 now leads with Annual manpower cost increase: the recommended database average, Lower allowance and Higher allowance, a selectable observed range with an average marker, and a synchronised exact percentage. Advanced yearly profiles follow the normal controls. The reference-method panel has been removed from Step 5 at the officer's request; underlying cohort records, statistics and provenance remain retained in the decision evidence. ## General reference cohort

Cohort general-fm-office-awarded-v1 is fixed independently of deployment titles: validated awarded Office FM records in SGD, inclusive monthly per head, expressly identified as manpower charges. The allowlisted general FM roles are Facilities Manager, Assistant Facilities Manager, Admin Executive, Technician, BMS Engineer, Property Officer, Environmental Engineer and Customer Service Officer. Submitted bids, other scopes, different rate bases/currencies and full trade/service prices are excluded.

Each distinct schedule contributes its median consecutive-year unit-rate movement. The arithmetic average and observed minimum/maximum summarise these schedule medians with equal schedule weights. At least three distinct schedules overall are required. Duplicate contract years are excluded; gaps and changed bases do not create inferred year-on-year pairs. Repeated deployment rows never add sample weight. Raw observations and excluded-pair issues remain inspectable. This explicitly replaces the requirement for three schedules matching every individual deployed role; it does not claim role-specific forecast evidence.

The current synthetic fixture yields 24 qualifying schedules, a displayed average of 3.17%, and a 2.50%-3.90% observed range. These are demonstration tender manpower charges, not actual market prices or employee wage increments. The selected allowance uses the existing two-decimal percentage convention; raw reference precision remains in the audit. No evidence fallback broadens this cohort. With insufficient evidence the interface offers no invented range or recommendation and accepts an explicit officer percentage, including zero.

The advanced yearly profile is now an editable table with contract year, base/option period and percentage. Year 1 remains fixed at zero. Existing PWM calculations, salary requirements and confirmations remain unchanged.
